# U4: NL's M3 optimizer vs AdamW (UERC)

*This file, `scripts/u4_report.py` and `scripts/dgx/make_u4_tasks.sh` were committed **before** any U4 run on UERC (2026-10-20). Results are appended below.*

## Why

Nested Learning has three families of ideas:

1. **memories**, tested in Phase 3 and U2: negative;
2. **multi-speed architectures / CMS**, tested in C3, C3b and U3: negative at scale;
3. **"deep optimizers"**, untested until now.

M3 (Multi-scale Momentum Muon, [NL] §7.2, Algorithm 1) is the paper's **only vision result**: better ImageNet-21K train and test loss than AdamW and Muon for ViTs. U4 asks the most direct form of the original question: **does NL's optimizer give a better ear-recognition model than AdamW?**

## Method (`nlear/m3.py`, tests in `tests/test_m3.py`)

**The update rule**, for every weight matrix (conv kernels viewed as matrices):

| | Update | When |
|---|---|---|
| Fast momentum | M1 ← β1·M1 + g | every step |
| Second moment | V ← β2·V + (1−β2)·g² | every step |
| **Slow momentum (the CMS level)** | M2 ← β3·M2 + Σg | **every f steps** |
| Weights | W ← W − lr·(NS(M1) + α·NS(M2)) / (√V̂ + ε) | every step |

**Choices we had to make**, because the paper gives no hyperparameter values:

- **Decay reading.** The paper writes the momenta without decay but calls the β's "momentum factors < 1". We use decaying momenta.
- **Constants:** β = (0.95, 0.999, 0.99), f = 16, NS = Muon's 5-step quintic iteration.
- **Muon convention:** M3 updates the body convolutions and the neck projection (24.5M parameters). AdamW keeps BN parameters, biases and the ArcFace head, at the U1 learning rates.
- **The CMS slow memory is the NL-specific part.** α = 0 removes it, which gives the **control**: the same optimizer without multi-scale momentum.

**Laptop probe** (EarVN, 2 epochs, val DIR@5%):

| Optimizer | Val DIR@5% |
|---|---|
| AdamW | 0.302 |
| M3, lr 1e-5 | 0.213 |
| M3, lr 1e-4 | 0.009 (fails) |

M3 needs a learning rate around 1e-5. It is also about 2.7× slower per step.

## Protocol

- **Everything is identical to U1** except the optimizer: ResNet-50 + ArcFace, 4 epochs, batch 64, AMP, same augmentation and schedule.
- **Seed 0 (validation only)** picks the learning rate separately for each family:
  - M3 (α = 1): lr ∈ {3e-6, 1e-5, 3e-5};
  - control (α = 0): lr ∈ {3e-6, 1e-5, 3e-5};
  - rule: best val DIR@5% (cosine_proto, 2 gallery images); a tie within 0.005 is broken by AUROC.
- **Seeds 1–3 (test)** run all 6 configurations in the same round. Only the two selected ones count.
- **AdamW reference:** the existing U1 runs, `runs_dgx/uerc/baseline_resnet50_e4/seed1..3`.
- **Limitation, stated in advance:** the training length is U1's 4 epochs, tuned for AdamW. M3 learns more slowly early on, so a longer schedule might favour it. Only the learning rate is tuned here.

## Decision rule

**Primary:** test DIR@5% (cosine_proto, 2 gallery images, all sources), paired over seeds 1–3.

- **GO (NL):** M3\* beats AdamW in **3/3** seeds **and** M3\* beats the control\* in **3/3** seeds. NL's optimizer helps, and the multi-scale (CMS) momentum is why.
- **PARTIAL:** M3\* beats AdamW in 3/3 seeds but not the control. A better optimizer, but not because of the NL part.
- **NO-GO:** M3\* does not beat AdamW in 3/3 seeds.

**Reported** (not decision criteria): rank-1, AUROC, EER, the 5-gallery variant, and the control vs AdamW.

## Amendment before reading any result (2026-10-21)

**M3 with α = 1 and lr 3e-5 diverged** (NaN weights, so evaluation failed) on all 4 seeds. This happened in both submissions (jobs 35022 and 35033; the second was an accidental resubmission of the same task list).

The pre-registered selection rule did not cover a configuration that produces no metrics. **Amendment:** a diverged configuration is ineligible for selection. It is decided only from the fact of divergence, before any U4 metric was read, and committed separately (`scripts/u4_report.py`).

### VERDICT: **NO-GO**

NL's M3 optimizer gives a clearly **worse** ear-recognition model than AdamW on every metric: −0.06 rank-1, −0.04 AUROC and −0.07 DIR@5%, consistently across seeds. **The NL-specific part (the slow, multi-scale momentum) makes it worse still**: M3 is below its own α = 0 control by 0.03 DIR@5%.

**Caveats (stated in advance):**

- The training length (4 epochs) was tuned for AdamW, and M3 learns more slowly early on, so only the learning rate was tuned.
- The M3 hyperparameters are our reading of an algorithm published without values.
- M3 was also unstable at lr 3e-5, and about 2.7× slower per step.

The result does **not** show that M3 cannot work for this task with a different schedule. It does show that, under a fair same-budget comparison, it is not a drop-in improvement over AdamW.

### Conclusion across all NL tests

All three families of NL ideas have now been tested under pre-registered rules. None improves on standard methods for open-set or continual ear recognition at scale:

- **Memories** (Phase 3, U2)
- **Multi-speed architectures / CMS** (C3, C3b, U3)
- **The NL optimizer** (U4)
