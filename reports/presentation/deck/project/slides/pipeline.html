Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

7

(45 seconds) Every model is trained the same way. An ear image is resized to 128 by 96, which keeps the ear’s shape, and goes through the ImageNet-pretrained backbone, which is fully fine-tuned. A small neck — dropout, a linear layer and batch norm — produces a 512-dimensional ear embedding. An ArcFace head turns that into a training loss with an angular margin, which pulls images of the same person together; it is used only for training. The five tasks are learned in turn: each task trains only on its own new people. The head adds the new people as they arrive, and only people seen so far compete. Each task gets a fixed number of optimiser steps with a warm-up and cosine decay. The joint model, trained on everyone at once with five times the steps, is the upper bound. The recipe is identical for every model; only the backbone learning rate and the step budget were tuned, once, on seed 0, and then frozen.
