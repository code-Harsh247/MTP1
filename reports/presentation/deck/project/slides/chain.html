Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

21

(60 seconds) Putting it together as one chain. One: on CNNs, CMS is in the right place — 93% of the forgetting is in its layers. Two: but its slow copies barely move, so they never learn the people. Three: when I make them learn at full strength, they forget like everything else. Four: even a perfect frozen memory is diluted in the blended output — 0.79 alone, 0.58 together. Five: and on most cells, most of the forgetting happens outside the CMS layers anyway. So the answers: RQ1, yes, forgetting is real and model-independent on large data. RQ2, no, CMS reduced it in zero of nine cells. RQ3, not yet: a working version must cover more of the network and let its memory actually reach the output.
