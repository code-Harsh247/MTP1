Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

19

(50 seconds) If the frozen copies are being outvoted, let’s give them more say. Normally the model chooses the mix itself, and it gave the two frozen copies 60% together. So after group 1, I fixed their say at 80%, and then 95%. On ConvNeXt it helps: group 1’s forgetting falls from 0.27 to 0.23 to 0.17. But there is a cost: the new people are learned worse, from 0.93 down to 0.87, because the model has less freedom to learn. And on DeiT, nothing changes at all. When I look inside the 95% model, what is still forgotten now sits outside CMS, in the neck and the rest of the backbone. That points to the real culprit.
