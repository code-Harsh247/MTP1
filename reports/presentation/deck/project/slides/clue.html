Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

16

(60 seconds) To find out why CMS failed, I looked inside the model, on EarVN ConvNeXt. First question: where is the forgetting? I took the plain model after all five groups and put back one part at a time to how it was after group 1. Putting back just the layers that CMS replaces brings back 93% of what group 1 lost. The neck brings back 29%, the rest of the backbone 18%. So the forgetting sits in exactly the layers CMS replaces: CMS is in the right place. Second question: do the slow copies actually learn? This chart shows how much each copy changed from its starting weights. The fast copy changes by about 10%. The slow copy changes by only about 1%. It barely learns the people at all, so it has nothing to keep.
