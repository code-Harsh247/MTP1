Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

2

(45 seconds) Think of a real ear recognition system. People are not enrolled all at once; new groups arrive over time. Each time a new group is added, the system must still recognise everyone enrolled before. And we should not have to retrain from scratch on everyone each time: that is slow and costly, and the old images may not even be kept. So each step updates the model with only the new group, but after step 3 it must still know groups 1, 2 and 3. Ears suit this well: they are stable with age and can be captured without contact.
