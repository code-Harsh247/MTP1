Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

Can Nested Learning’s memory help a model keep the people it has already learned?

Harsh Chattar · 22CS30028

Supervisor: Prof. Debasis Samanta · Mentor: Santhoshkumar Peddi

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Kharagpur

(15 seconds) Good morning. My project asks whether a new idea from Nested Learning can stop a biometric system, here ear recognition, from forgetting the people they have already enrolled. I will first show that the forgetting is real, then test Nested Learning’s memory against it, and spend most of the time on why it did not work.
