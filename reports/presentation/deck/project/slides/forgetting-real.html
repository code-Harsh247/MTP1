Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

9

(55 seconds) The answer to RQ1 is yes. I used a strict rule, fixed before the runs: on every seed, forgetting must be at least 0.10, and the final accuracy must be at least 0.10 below the model trained on everyone at once. EarVN has 3 seeds, the other datasets 5, and one failing seed is enough to rule a cell out. It is shown in 9 of the 20 model–dataset cells. On EarVN, the largest dataset, all five families forget heavily, with rank-1 forgetting between 0.33 and 0.48. AWE shows it for three families and AMI for one. IITD shows none, because accuracy there is near the ceiling and hides it. Whether the model is a CNN or a transformer does not matter. On the right is one accuracy matrix: each row is the model after learning one more task. Read down the first column: task 1 starts at 0.88 and ends at 0.46.
