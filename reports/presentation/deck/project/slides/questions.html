Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

3

(35 seconds) Nobody had measured forgetting in continual ear recognition carefully, across several datasets and model families. So I ask three questions. RQ1: is forgetting real here? RQ2: does Nested Learning’s memory, CMS, reduce it? And RQ3: if not, why not, and what would it take? The talk follows these three in order.
