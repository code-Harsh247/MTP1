How the verdicts were checked

  • Each stage’s design, rule and report script committed before its runs
  • Verdicts produced by the committed scripts
  • Every verdict recomputed from the raw accuracy matrices by a separate script: no mismatches
  • No rule relaxed after its results were seen
  • All runs on Kaggle T4 GPUs; 1017 training runs in total
