Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

6

(45 seconds) How do I measure forgetting? First, the accuracy on one task. Each person in the task is enrolled from a few photos the model never trained on, using the current model. Then the model is shown other unseen photos of those people and names the closest one. Accuracy is the share it gets right. On EarVN, a task has 23 people and about 750 test photos. Second, I repeat this after every task. Here is task 1: 0.88 right after it is learned, then it falls as new groups arrive, ending at 0.46. Third, forgetting is the best score minus the final score: 0.88 minus 0.46 is 0.42. I average that over tasks 1 to 4; task 5 is left out because nothing comes after it.
