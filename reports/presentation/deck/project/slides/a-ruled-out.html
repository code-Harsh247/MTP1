Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Ear Recognition

17

(60 seconds) So why do the slow copies barely learn? The first suspect: their updates are too small. The optimiser keeps every update about the same size, so a slow copy waits 256 steps and then takes just one normal step. It learns about 1/256 of what it should. To test this, I gave the slow copies full-size updates: all 256 steps’ worth, applied at once. And it worked: the slow copies now change by 15%, even more than the fast copy. But look at the table. Forgetting got worse, not better: higher than with no CMS at all, on every run. The reason: once the slow copy learns fully, it also learns the new people, and forgets the old ones just like everything else. Updating less often doesn’t protect anything. So suspect A is ruled out. Which raises the next question: what if a slow copy kept the old people perfectly?
