(45 seconds) Evaluation happens after every task, and the head is not used. Each person who has been trained on is enrolled again with the current model: their gallery images are embedded and averaged into one cosine prototype. Held-out probe images, which were never trained on, are matched to the nearest prototype, and probes from people never trained on are mixed in to test open-set rejection. The rank-1 accuracy on task j after training on task t fills one entry of a 5 by 5 accuracy matrix. The table shows what is held out per dataset: on EarVN, 23 people per task, with 3,732 held-out probes. IITD has too few images per person, so its training images serve as the gallery. Rank-1 is the primary metric; open-set detection rates and AUROC are also reported. The key point: because everyone is re-enrolled with the current model, forgetting here means the embedding itself has drifted, which is exactly what matters in a deployed system.
