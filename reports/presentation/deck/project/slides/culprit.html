(60 seconds) Finally, I ran the frozen-copies test on all nine model and dataset pairs. Group 1’s forgetting drops everywhere, the green bars are always lower, but a lot of forgetting remains. Overall, freezing cut forgetting enough to count in only 2 of the 9 pairs. So I took four of these models apart, putting one part back at a time, like on slide 16. Only on ConvNeXt is most of the lost accuracy inside the CMS layers. On ResNet, DeiT and Swin, most of it is outside CMS, in the rest of the network. That is the real culprit: most forgetting happens in parts CMS doesn’t cover. Even a perfect frozen copy there can’t stop it.
